Shoot, shoot, shoot like crazy!! The invincible robot Bangai-O launches to bring down the SF Cosmo Gang, the crime syndicate terrorising space! Its pilot is Riki, a hero burning with a sense of justice. Together with his sister Mami he unleashes outrageously flashy attacks. Enemies that come, enemies that do not - every one of them is an enemy. Let fly with missiles until they fill the screen, and win back peace for space!!
